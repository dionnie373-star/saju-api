3. Purpose and legal basis

We process your birth details to give you the profile you request (performance of a service you ask for; Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, where GDPR applies). We count anonymous visits and clicks to improve the service (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Service providers

  • Render Services, Inc. (USA) hosts our website and application. When you visit the site, the hosting provider creates technical server logs (for example IP address, time of the request, requested path and status code). We do not store these logs ourselves. The provider keeps them for a short time and then deletes them automatically. Data may be transferred to the USA under standard contractual clauses.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com, Switzerland) provides the geocoding service we use to find the time zone of your place of birth. If your place is not in our built-in list, our server sends the place name you typed (not your IP address) to this service.
  • The instant profile on this page is calculated on our own server. It does not use artificial intelligence and no birth details are sent to an AI provider.

5. How long we keep data

We do not keep the birth details you enter for the instant profile. Emails you send us are kept until your request is finished.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, to object, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority. Write to dionnie373@gmail.com to use these rights.

7. Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies or third-party analytics or tracking tools. To understand how the service is used, we count visits and button clicks anonymously on our own server. We store only the date, the source marker from the link (for example “ig”, “tt” or “yt”), the page path and the type of event, as counters. We do not store IP addresses or browser identifiers in these counters, so we cannot recognise individual people. To protect the service from abuse, our server briefly keeps your IP address in memory (up to one hour) to limit the number of requests. For each profile request we also save one anonymous row with a timestamp, the source marker and the language, without any birth details. If a link contains a source marker or the “nohit” option, your browser may save that marker in its session or local storage on your device. The fonts on our pages are served from our own server, so your browser does not connect to Google or other third parties for fonts.

8. Emails and marketing

We do not send newsletters or advertising emails. You only receive an email from us if you write to us first.
