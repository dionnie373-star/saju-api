Yin and Yang in Saju

Yin and Yang are not a good-versus-bad scheme but two forms in which the same thing appears. In Saju each of the five elements gets a Yang form and a Yin form.

Two forms per element

The ten Heavenly Stems arise because each of the five elements appears twice: once as Yang (outward, open, strong), once as Yin (inward, fine, condensed).

ElementYangYin
Wood甲 (large tree)乙 (grass, flower)
Fire丙 (sun)丁 (candle flame)
Earth戊 (mountain)己 (field)
Metal庚 (axe, sword)辛 (jewelry, needle)
Water壬 (sea, river)癸 (rain, dew)

The images in brackets are the usual memory aids of the tradition. They help with remembering; they are not a fixed description of character.

Yin and Yang in the years

Years are also Yin or Yang. Years that end in an even number carry Yang stems (2026: 丙, Yang Fire). Years ending in an odd digit are Yin (2027: 丁, Yin Fire). That is why 2026 is often described as “open fire” and 2027 as “quieter fire”. See Year of the Fire Sheep 2027.

How you can use this

As a question: where in my life may I go outward right now, and where more inward? It is no more than a thinking model, and it does not replace advice.
