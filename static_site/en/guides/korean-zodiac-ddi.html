The Ddi in everyday life

In Korea the question “What is your Ddi?” is a popular conversation opener. People say things like “I'm a Tiger Ddi” (호랑이띠). When your own animal returns in the new year, it is called the Ddi year (띠해), traditionally associated with looking back and self-examination.
