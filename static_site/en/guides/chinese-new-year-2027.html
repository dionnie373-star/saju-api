Chinese New Year 2027

Chinese New Year 2027 falls on 6 February and begins the year of the Sheep. Here is how it fits with Korean Seollal and the Saju year (Lichun).

Date and animal

Lunar New Year 2027 is on 6 February 2027. It begins the year 丁未, the Fire Sheep. The date changes every year because it follows the lunar calendar and falls between the end of January and mid-February.

And Korean Seollal?

In Korea the festival is called Seollal (설날). It also follows the lunar calendar and usually falls on the same day as Chinese New Year. Family visits, the ancestral bow Sebae (세배) and the soup Tteokguk (떡국) are customary.

Why Saju counts differently

Saju does not count the year from New Year but from Lichun (around 4 February). In 2027 Lichun therefore falls two days before Lunar New Year. Someone born between these days can have a different animal in everyday horoscopes than in Saju. More on this: How to calculate your Chinese zodiac sign.
