How to calculate your Chinese zodiac sign

The animal of your birth year does not depend on 1 January. In Saju what counts is Lichun, the start of spring around 4 February. Here you learn the simple calculation and the exceptions.

The simple calculation

The twelve animals repeat in a fixed order: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Sheep, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig. If you divide the year minus 4 by 12, the remainder shows your animal (0 = Rat, 1 = Ox, … 11 = Pig). Example: 1990 − 4 = 1986, and 1986 divided by 12 leaves remainder 6, so Horse.

The exception: the year begins at Lichun

This only applies if you were born after Lichun. In Saju the new year begins at the solar point Lichun, usually on 3, 4 or 5 February. Anyone born before it calculates with the previous year. So someone born on 20 January 1990 has the animal of 1989 in Saju, the Snake.

And Lunar New Year?

Chinese or Korean New Year falls between the end of January and mid-February and is often used in everyday life as the turn of the year. Many popular horoscopes therefore use it. Saju, by contrast, counts from Lichun. Both are established conventions, which is why two sources can name two different animals for the same person.

Birthdays near the boundary

If you were born between mid-January and mid-February, a day-exact calculation is worthwhile. The exact moment of Lichun shifts by a few hours every year. Palja calculates with the actual solar points.
