Chinese zodiac 2027: the year of the Fire Sheep

2027 is the year 丁未 (Jeongmi) in the Chinese and Korean calendars: the Fire Sheep (in English the animal is also called Goat or Ram). Here you can find out when this year really begins, what the signs mean and how the twelve animals traditionally relate to the Sheep.

What does 丁未 mean?

In the East Asian calendar every year has two characters: a Heavenly Stem (ten possibilities) and an Earthly Branch (twelve possibilities, the animals). For 2027 the combination is 丁未.

  • 丁 (Ding) stands for Yin Fire: the quiet flame, warmth, expression, light on a small scale.
  • 未 (Wei) is the Sheep. Its associated element is Earth, and it stands for late summer.

This gives the image of the “Fire Sheep”: Fire and Earth, warmth on solid ground. How much to read into such an image is a matter of interpretation.

When does the year really begin?

The Saju year begins neither on 1 January nor at Lunar New Year, but at Lichun (立春, “start of spring”), around 4 February. Someone born in January 2027 belongs to the animal of the previous year, which is the Horse (2026). Someone born in December has the animal of the current year. The exact time of Lichun changes from year to year, so people with a birthday in early February should calculate carefully.

Why your animal is only one part

The animal of your birth year is only one of eight characters (hence the Korean name Palja, 팔자, “eight characters”). Saju has four pillars: year, month, day and hour, each with one Heavenly Stem and one Earthly Branch. The day pillar is regarded as your “self”. Anyone who knows only the animal knows only part of the picture.
